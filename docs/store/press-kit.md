## 2. About (boilerplate)

**50 words**
> Last Lantern is a 3D survivors-like where your light is your life. Every hit dims your lantern: the warm light
> around you shrinks and the Gloom creeps closer. Build absurd weapon combinations, survive hundreds of shadows at
> once, and face the master of the night at minute fifteen.

**100 words**
> The sun has not risen in a hundred years, and one lantern still burns. In **Last Lantern** you carry it through
> three moonlit nights full of shadows. Your weapons fire on their own. Your job is to move, choose upgrades and
> survive. Your health *is* your light: every hit shrinks the warm pool around you and lets the violet Gloom close
> in, and lamp oil pushes it back. Combine 11 weapons and 14 relics, evolve them into absurd new forms, unlock six
> bearers, and spend Ash at the Hearth between runs. It's a storybook world with real teeth, built for "one more
> run."

## 3. Key features
- **Your light is your life.** Health is a radius, not just a bar. Taking damage makes the world smaller.
- **Builds that break the night.** 11 weapons, each with an evolution. 14 relics. Reroll, skip and banish.
- **300+ enemies, never muddy.** Readable silhouettes, warm-vs-cool colour coding, and a flash, sound and particle
  burst on every hit.
- **Three nights, three bosses.** The Hollow Stag, the Mire Mother and the Ashen King, then an endless night if you dare.
- **One more run.** 6 bearers, 13 permanent Hearth upgrades, 24 achievements that unlock new gear.
- **A moonlit storybook look.** Chunky, rounded models, saturated blue-violet nights, soft real-time shadows,
  ambient occlusion and wind-blown grass. It runs at 60 fps on modest laptops.
- **Play anywhere.** Free web demo in the browser. Offline desktop build with full gamepad support.

## 4. Development story (for interviews / devlog #1)
Last Lantern was built by a small human-directed team working with an **AI game studio**: Claude Code agents in
the roles of designer, programmers, artists, sound designer, QA and producer, organised with the open-source
*Claude Code Game Studios* template. Every 3D model is generated by Blender Python scripts. Every sound is
synthesised from scratch, with no sample libraries. The art direction is an original "moonlit storybook" style.
We're open about how the game was made: the AI disclosure is on every store page. Angle for press: *"What does a
polished, sellable indie game look like when an AI studio builds it under human direction?"*

## 5. Logo & key art brief
- **Wordmark:** "LAST" in small tracked caps over a heavy rounded "LANTERN", with an amber-to-gold vertical
  gradient, a dark violet outline (`#1a1040`) and a soft amber outer glow. It matches the in-game title screen
  (font: Lilita One, OFL). Deliver as transparent PNG at 1280×720 and 3840×2160, plus SVG.
- **Icon / emblem:** the moon-and-lantern emblem, `electron/assets/icon.svg` (squircle app icon) and the
  title-screen emblem in `src/ui/art.ts`.
- **Key art composition (all capsules crop from one 4K master):** the hooded bearer (Wren, magenta robe) on a
  grassy rise, lantern held high on her staff. A warm amber pool of light covers the lower third. Out of the
  violet fog behind her come a ring of shade silhouettes with glowing eyes and the antlered silhouette of the
  Hollow Stag. A huge pale moon sits upper-left. Palette: `#120b2e` night, `#2b1a78` violet, `#0f6e86` teal,
  `#ffb13a` lantern amber, `#ffe7a0` highlight.
- **Production (done, re-runnable):** `node tools/package-screens.mjs` renders the key-art plate **in the engine**
  (the title screen's low hero camera, mid-vigil, ~220 shadows advancing out of the fog under the moon) and composes
  every capsule with the shipping logo markup and fonts. Output: `production/release/store-art/capsules/`
  (`key-art-1920x1080.png`, `logo-1280x720.png` transparent, all Steam/itch sizes). See `docs/store/README.md`.
- **Optional upgrade:** a painted or Blender-posed 4K master (EEVEE render of `public/models/characters/bearer.glb` +
  `public/models/enemies/*.glb`, using `plates/keyart.png` as the layout reference). Do not paint over other games'
  assets or styles.
