## Art & audio
- All 3D models are original, generated by Blender Python scripts in `tools/blender/`.
- All music and sound effects are original, synthesised from scratch by `tools/audio/` (no samples, no sound libraries).
- The visual style ("moonlit storybook") is original. No third-party characters, assets, logos or trademarks are used.
